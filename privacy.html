Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

Overview

DevSecure Lab is a free, self-paced educational website. No account or login is required to read the learning content.

Local browser storage

The site stores completed lesson identifiers and theme preference in your browser using localStorage. This information is used only to display progress on your device. It is not sent to a DevSecure Lab server.

Hosting logs

GitHub Pages or other hosting infrastructure may maintain standard access and security logs, such as IP address, browser and request time. Those logs are controlled by the hosting provider.

Cookies and analytics

DevSecure Lab does not intentionally use advertising cookies, marketing trackers or third-party analytics in this static release.

External resources

Course pages link to official documentation from organizations such as OWASP, NIST, MDN, Python and Linux documentation. External websites have their own privacy policies and practices.

Changes

Policy changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions, contact the project maintainer through the official GitHub repository.
