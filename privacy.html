Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

DevSecure Lab is committed to user privacy. We believe in building a privacy-first educational platform. We do not require account registration, logins, or the submission of personally identifiable information (PII) to access our learning modules.

2. Local Storage Usage

Our interactive progress tracking system operates strictly on your local web browser using localStorage API. The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Completed lesson identifiers to calculate your course progress.
  • Theme preferences (Dark Mode vs. Light Mode selection).

Note: This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

3. Cookies and Analytics

DevSecure Lab does not store tracking cookies, third-party marketing trackers, or aggressive telemetry scripts. If hosted on platforms like GitHub Pages, standard web server access logs (such as IP addresses and User-Agents) may be maintained by the host provider for security and service stability.

4. External Links & Resources

Our platform links to third-party authoritative security websites (e.g., OWASP, MDN, NIST, Python Docs) for further reading. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage learners to review the privacy policies of external sites upon visiting them.

5. Updates & Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform enhancements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or DevSecure Lab's privacy architecture, you can contact us via our official repository or platform support channels.
