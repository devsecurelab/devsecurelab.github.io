Responsible Use

DevSecure Lab teaches defensive skills. You must use every lesson only on systems you own or have written permission to assess.

Required boundaries

  • Define scope, timing, allowed methods and an emergency contact before testing.
  • Use disposable, local or explicitly authorized labs whenever possible.
  • Do not access, alter, disrupt, exfiltrate or persist in systems without permission.
  • Do not use lessons to obtain credentials, evade controls or target real people.
  • Handle evidence minimally and remove sensitive data from reports.

Responsible disclosure

If you find a vulnerability in an authorized system, follow the owner’s disclosure process. Do not publish sensitive details before a coordinated resolution.

Educational limitation

Content is educational and does not grant authorization. You are responsible for your actions, applicable law and the rules of each lab or service.
