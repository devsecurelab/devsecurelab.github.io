Safety first

Responsible Use Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

Authorization is required

DevSecure Lab provides cybersecurity education for defensive learning and authorized security testing only. Never test a system, network, account or application unless you own it or have explicit written permission from the system owner.

Use an isolated lab

Use localhost, disposable virtual machines, intentionally vulnerable training applications and private lab networks. Keep snapshots and rollback plans before testing code.

Define scope

Written authorization should identify targets, exclusions, permitted methods, testing windows, emergency contacts and data-handling rules.

Prohibited activity

Do not use this material for unauthorized scanning, credential theft, malware deployment, persistence, evasion, service disruption, data exfiltration or attacks against public systems.

Responsible disclosure

If you discover a vulnerability in a system you are authorized to assess, preserve evidence carefully, notify the owner through the agreed channel and provide practical remediation guidance.

Learner responsibility

You are responsible for complying with applicable laws, contracts and organizational policies. This educational policy is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
